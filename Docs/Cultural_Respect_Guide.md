The Starfield Intelligent Gallery — Cultural & Linguistic Respect Guide
Version 1.0 — June 2, 2026

🌍 1. Purpose of This Guide
The Starfield Intelligent Gallery (SIG) is used by travelers from every culture, every language, every identity, and every corner of the world. This guide ensures that SIG honors those differences with dignity, accuracy, and respect.

This is not a translation manual.
This is not a localization checklist.

This is a cultural covenant — a promise that SIG will never harm, exclude, or misrepresent the people who use it.

🌐 2. Core Principles of Cultural Respect
2.1 Humanity First
Every user is a human being with:

A history

A culture

A language

A worldview

A lived experience

SIG must treat every user with the same dignity it would offer a fellow traveler in the Settled Systems.

2.2 No Culture Is “Default”
SIG rejects the idea of a “default” culture, language, or identity.

This means:

No UI text assumes a specific cultural background

No symbolism is used without understanding its meaning

No phrasing implies one worldview is “normal” and others are deviations

The universe is vast.
SIG reflects that vastness.

2.3 Language Is Identity
When SIG translates, it must:

Preserve meaning

Preserve tone

Preserve dignity

Avoid stereotypes

Avoid literal translations that distort cultural nuance

A translation is not a mirror — it is a bridge.

2.4 Avoid Harmful or Exclusionary Content
SIG must never include:

Cultural stereotypes

Offensive idioms

Harmful metaphors

Slurs or reclaimed terms used incorrectly

Gender‑exclusive phrasing where unnecessary

Imagery with negative cultural connotations

If a phrase could be misinterpreted, SIG chooses clarity and kindness.

🌈 3. LGBTQ+ Respect & Inclusion
SIG explicitly protects LGBTQ+ users.

This includes:

Using inclusive language

Avoiding gender assumptions

Respecting pronouns

Avoiding heteronormative framing

Ensuring translations do not erase LGBTQ+ identities

Avoiding imagery or phrasing that could be interpreted as discriminatory

SIG is a place where identity is safe.

🧠 4. Neurodivergent Cultural Respect
Neurodivergence is not just a cognitive difference — it is a cultural identity for many.

SIG respects:

Autistic communication styles

ADHD expression patterns

Dyslexic reading preferences

Sensory‑sensitive needs

Direct communication

Non‑linear thought

Stimming behaviors (in metaphorical UI form)

SIG never pathologizes.
SIG never diminishes.
SIG never “corrects” the user’s natural style.

🌎 5. Global Linguistic Guidelines
SIG supports a multilingual universe.
To ensure cultural respect, translations must follow these rules:

5.1 Preserve Meaning, Not Just Words
Avoid literal translations that distort cultural nuance.

Example:
A phrase like “capture your journey” may need different metaphors in different languages.

5.2 Avoid Idioms That Don’t Travel
Idioms often fail across cultures.

SIG avoids:

“Break a leg”

“Hit the ground running”

“Out of the blue”

Instead, SIG uses universal metaphors:

Light

Motion

Memory

Discovery

Stars

5.3 Respect Formality Levels
Some languages require:

Formal pronouns

Honorifics

Politeness markers

Cultural humility

SIG must adapt to each language’s social structure.

5.4 Avoid Colonial or Imperial Framing
SIG must never imply:

Cultural superiority

Western‑centric norms

Erasure of indigenous identities

“Civilized vs. uncivilized” metaphors

The Gallery is for all travelers — not just one worldview.

🎨 6. Visual & Symbolic Respect
Symbols carry cultural meaning.
SIG must avoid:

Religious symbols

Political symbols

Nationalistic imagery

Colors with strong cultural connotations (unless intentional and respectful)

Gestures that may be offensive in some regions

SIG’s visual language should feel universal, mythic, and timeless.

🔧 7. Developer Guidelines
All SIG contributors must:

Research cultural implications of UI text

Avoid slang and idioms

Use gender‑neutral language where possible

Provide context notes for translators

Avoid culturally charged symbols

Test UI in multiple languages

Consider reading direction (LTR/RTL)

Avoid humor that relies on cultural assumptions

Respect is not optional — it is required.

🌌 8. The Cultural Respect Oath
Every contributor to SIG implicitly agrees to this oath:

“I will honor every traveler’s culture, language, and identity.
I will design with humility, empathy, and respect.
I will ensure the Gallery welcomes all who enter.”

This oath is part of SIG’s soul.

⭐ 9. Conclusion — A Universe of Many Voices
The Starfield Intelligent Gallery is not just a tool.
It is a meeting place of cultures, identities, and minds.

This guide ensures that SIG remains:

Respectful

Inclusive

Culturally aware

Linguistically sensitive

Emotionally safe

The universe is vast.
The Gallery must be just as vast in its respect.
